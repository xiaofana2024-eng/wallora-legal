Wallora Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Wallora is a local wallpaper and home-screen setup archive app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we store on your device

Setup titles, notes, device aliases, image copies you attach, optional dictated note text, purchase receipts metadata, and preference flags are stored locally in the app sandbox. Wallora does not require an account.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap Take Photo.
  • Photo Library — only when you choose or save photos.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only when you start dictation.
  • App Tracking Transparency and Notifications — system prompts may appear on first home entry. This build does not use tracking identifiers for ads and does not send push or local notification campaigns after the prompt.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Wallora stores verified transaction identifiers and granted pack credits on device for entitlement bookkeeping.

No sale of personal data

We do not sell personal information. Core archive features work offline.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support entry configured in the App Store listing for Wallora.
